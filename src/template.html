Unofficial student project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Texas A&M University or Transportation Services. No sign-in and no tracking; if you use your location, it stays on your phone.

Map data, walkways and Aggie Spirit route lines © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Community-mapped routes can lag behind current service, so check the official Aggie Spirit app before you ride; where a mapped route had breaks, the missing stretch follows the shortest road path, checked against City of College Station and TAMU bus-road layers. Building heights from USGS 3DEP lidar flown in 2018 (buildings newer than that use estimates). Tree canopy from High Resolution Canopy Height Maps v2 by Meta and WRI (CC BY 4.0), resampled to 2 m; it can miss recent plantings or removals and treats each crown as solid shade. Off campus, bus-ride shade is precomputed in a 150 m band along each route for sun positions in 15° × 10° steps. Shade estimates are a guide, not a heat-safety tool: carry water and take breaks.
